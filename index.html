Our Mission Statement

We strive to create reliable and intuitive applications for our customers that provide solutions they truly value for the advancement of their businesses. Through our team's focused collaboration, the customer's needs are our priority.

Team Rules:

We will always be honest with each other; we will always help each other when someone needs help; we will always update each other when something happens or if we add something to the project; and we will always keep on top of communication. If we are falling behind, we keep up with communication, extend or add meeting times, and ask for help from the teacher. If someone is weighing the team down, we will see what is weighing them down and determine if it is their fault or if it is something else.
